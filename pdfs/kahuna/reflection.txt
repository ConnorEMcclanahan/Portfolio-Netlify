Begining this project was hard at first we didnt know what direction to bring a weather app, since there are so many out there we needed to find our niche. First we set out to do interviews and get feedback from our teachers and the people around us, after not really having a good plan I began making a competitor analysis to get some more ideas about the diffrent directions we could bring our site. At this point we commited on making a app for surfers because it seemed to be the most intresting and unique idea we could find.

Once we decided on our idea we changed it during the creation of our POV to target more of the beginner audiance since all the exisiting surfing apps and websites are more about the data and less about the user experiance. This was perfect and I think that we achived our goal even if there is more that can be done to improve it. Throughout this process we checked back with our teachers and got feedback this can be seen in our feedpulse.

Overall I feel quite happy with the results of this IOS section. Everthing we did was intresting and I feel like I have grown as a programmer and designer. Working with IOS was difficult at first but as we got into it go easier and I notied just how user friendly everything is from XCode to Swift it was quite intuitive. Im looking forward to learning how to program with new languages in the future.
