About Me

I'm a California native and Dutch-trained designer and developer, recently earning my BSc in ICT Media & Design from Fontys University in Eindhoven (2021–2026). Moving across the world taught me how to adapt quickly and design with deep empathy for diverse audiences.

I don't just hand off Figma mockups and walk away. My sweet spot is bridging the gap between research and production-ready code. Whether I'm building an accessible Bluetooth ECG app for elderly patients at Diplora that passed rigorous user validation, enforcing strict WCAG accessibility standards from day one, or deploying rugged PWAs directly onto factory floors for Motivate.nl, I focus on software built to survive the messy real world.

That pragmatic approach to stripping away hype and building genuinely useful, accessible interfaces stems from my minor in AI for Society, and it drives everything I build.

When I'm away from the screen, you'll usually find me cooking or exploring city architecture. Years of restaurant work taught me how to thrive under pressure in a tight-knit team—a collaborative, high-energy mentality I bring directly to developers, researchers, and stakeholders.
